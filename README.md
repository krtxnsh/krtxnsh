I'm a CSE student who learns best by building. Most of my projects start as a problem I run into myself, like sharing rides across campus or keeping memories from fading, and grow from there.

Right now I'm sharpening my fundamentals through **Data Structures & Algorithms**, going deeper into **AI/ML**, and levelling up as a **full-stack developer**. I enjoy hackathons because they force fast decisions, tight scope, and a demo that actually works.

Outside of code, I design in Figma and edit video in Premiere Pro, which helps me care about how things look as well as how they run.
